About

Philipe Pereira is an École Polytechnique de Paris graduate with 15 years of experience in computing. He has delivered more than a dozen applications in real-time power system dispatching, cable modeling, power flow analysis and state estimation. He worked at Cemig Distribuicão in NEO-COD departament (Operation Efficiency Division - Distribuition Operation Center) and he is currently working at OPAL-RT Technologies in eFPGASIM division.
